Section 01
Overview

This project introduces a sleek, focused retail sales analysis system built to track item-level performance metrics for premium footwear inventories. The dashboard breaks down active listings, pricing gaps, customer feedback loops, and volume numbers for individual product styles. This allows stock managers to track item metrics down to specific colorways and sizing availability rules.

Section 02
Business Problem

In retail, managing high-ticket release products can be tricky. Sizing demands and pricing differences often cause stock issues. Without item-level tracking, managers struggle to see if listing prices match actual final sales, whether customer satisfaction is dropping on high-volume sizes, or which specific product options are stuck in storage instead of driving retail revenue.

Section 03
Methodology

The retail dashboard architecture followed three simple steps:
• Data Extraction & SKU Mapping: Loaded raw product transaction databases into the data model. Organized rows by unique item codes (SKUs) and size variables to allow targeted product audits.
• Dropdown Drilldown Design: Created a smooth filter flow so picking a single shoe option instantly switches out the product image, descriptive block, and item-level revenue cards across the board.
• Simple Performance Formulas:
  - Sizing Sales Volume is calculated as: The total aggregate count of units sold within a distinct size partition.
  - Listing Pricing Variance is calculated as: The stated listing price minus the final retail transaction price to track profit margin drops.

Section 07
Conclusion

In conclusion, setting up an interactive item-level dashboard gives stock managers complete control over product launches. Tracking size distributions proves that inventory choices must remain heavily skewed toward core sizes like 10.0 to match real-world demand patterns. Moving forward, this clean, plain layout model can be rolled out across wider inventory categories to maintain zero price discounting and optimize general supply pipelines.
