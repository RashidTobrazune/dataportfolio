Section 01
Overview

This project is an interactive dashboard built to track waste and recycling across 15 different areas. The system connects daily garbage collection data with environmental impact scores. This helps managers easily view total tons collected, check the quality of recycled materials, track funding money, and see how many people attend community green events.

Section 02
Business Problem

Before this dashboard was built, waste data was a mess. Garbage numbers, station processing speeds, and carbon savings were hidden away in separate files. Because the data was disconnected, managers could not see which stations were slow, where trash was getting mixed up with recycling (about 9.76% is contaminated), or if the company was meeting its main goal of recycling at least 55.0% of all collected waste.

Section 03
Methodology

The project was built using three simple steps:
• Data Cleaning (ETL): Collected raw data tables from different trash stations. Used Power Query to remove blank spaces, fix errors, and format everything into clean lists of numbers.
• Environmental Math: Used standard formulas to turn the weight of recycled materials into green points. For example, we calculated energy savings based on the final weight of reused materials.
• Simple Formulas:
  - The Recycling Rate is calculated as: Total Tons Recycled divided by Total Tons Collected, multiplied by 100.
  - Carbon Savings per Ton is calculated as: Total Tons of CO2 Saved divided by Total Tons of Recycled Waste.

Section 04
Key Insights
📈

Beat the Recycling Target

The system shows a final recycling rate of 61.58%. This beats the company target of 55.0%, which keeps the project eligible for future funding grants.

⚡

Massive Energy Savings

Recycled waste saved 1.73M kWh of energy. The charts show that April was the best month of the year, saving 233K kWh of electricity.

⚠️

Slow Station Bottlenecks

The time charts found a big delay at the South Transfer Station. It takes an average of 106.09 minutes to process waste there because the equipment is too slow.

Section 07
Conclusion

In short, bringing old spreadsheet files into a live Power BI dashboard completely changed how we manage waste. Reaching a final recycling rate of 61.58% proves our green programs work. Next, the company can focus on fixing the slow processing times at the South Transfer Station and teaching citizens how to properly sort items to lower our 9.76% trash contamination rate.
