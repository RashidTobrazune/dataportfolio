Section 01
Overview

This project introduces a centralized retention tracking dashboard built to analyze customer attrition (churn) trends across various account pools. By linking internal database files, the tool maps out how subscription choices, account payment habits, and customer service reviews impact real-world brand loyalty. This provides clear tracking to protect company revenue lines and build long-term subscriber relationships.

Section 02
Business Problem

The company faced a clear operational challenge: losing active subscribers to market competitors. Because data was split into separate logging systems, it was incredibly difficult to trace why users were leaving, which contract groups were at risk, or how much profit was escaping. Without dashboard visibility, management could not plan customer loyalty offers or fix service friction points—especially for high-spending target customer segments who make up a huge chunk of company turnover.

Section 03
Methodology

The project pipeline was executed using three straightforward steps:
• Data Preparation: Cleaned the raw data blocks provided by checking for matching duplicate rows and replacing missing entries with "Unknown" labels.
• Data Transformation: Normalized customer lists, sorted spending groups, and built calculated columns to isolate active counts against final losses.
• Mathematical Rules:
  - Churn Rate is calculated as: The number of churned customers divided by the total customer base, multiplied by 100.
  - High-Spending Churn Concentration is calculated as: Churned high-spending users divided by the global churn volume, multiplied by 100.

Section 04
Key Insights
⚠️

Early Lifecycles Face High Attrition

Subscribers with accounts active for less than one year make up a staggering 53.56% of total losses, mostly leaving to take up better deals from competitors.

💳

Payment Policy Vulnerability

Customers using direct debit payment choices held the highest churn risk profile at 71.10%, pointing to immediate policy review requirements.

💎

High-Value Revenue Leaks

High-spending accounts represent 590 individual departures. This makes up 32.85% of total losses, threatening top-tier revenue margins.

Section 07
Conclusion

In summary, organizing raw subscriber data into live Power BI charts changes how we protect user relationships. The dashboard proves that targeting early account sign-ups with competitive price deals and fixing direct debit billing issues can safely lower customer turnover. Moving forward, launching custom loyalty rewards for high-spending tiers can safeguard high-value revenue margins across consecutive financial quarters.
