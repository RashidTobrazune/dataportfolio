Section 01
Overview

This programmatic system provides an interactive, three-page executive operational framework built to capture critical data flows across Rashid Cafeteria, Eatery & Cafe. Spanning a three-month audit window from January to March, the reporting metrics unify high-level finance metrics, transactional itemized engineering, and real-time backend kitchen fulfillment workflows into a single system of truth.

Section 02
Business Problem

Prior to deploying this analytics layout, management struggled to isolate margin erosion and service bottlenecks. Financial registers were disconnected from floor kitchen fulfillment monitors. This blinded decision-makers to massive discrepancies—such as a critical service friction delay during weekends where average prep times spiked to 25.8 minutes, resulting in over 13,500 slow orders that risked customer loyalty despite generating healthy cash flows.

Section 03
Methodology

The analytics pipeline was built through three systematic stages:
• Data Engineering & Normalization: Raw point-of-sale logs were normalized within Power Query to reconcile localized currency parameters (GH₵) and structural menu groups. Uncleaned transaction logs were standardized into consistent category clusters (Beverages, Continental, Grill & Starters, Local Ghanaian).
• Temporal Peak Logic: Orders were programmatically split into operational windows to map structural variables, explicitly isolating Peak Revenue runs from Off-Peak streams, alongside deep metrics covering weekday vs. weekend shifts.
• Core Formulas Applied:
  - Profit Margin % = (Total Profit ÷ Total Revenue) × 100
  - Beverage Attachment % = (Beverage Transactions ÷ Total Orders Placed) × 100

07
Strategic Conclusion

In summary, while the operational data proves that Rashid Cafeteria maintains an exceptional margin baseline (65.3%), it highlights clear infrastructure limitations. Future development resources should prioritize staff optimization and kitchen preparation re-engineering during high-stress evening windows (7 PM – 10 PM) on weekends. Introducing prep-stage automations or pre-batching high-volume assets like signature beverages will systematically minimize service friction and lower the 25.8-minute average latency down to optimal parameters.
