    The story behind the maps

    Engineer by training, and maps are where it clicked for me. Give me any dataset and my first question is always the same: where? Where is the city growing, where will the water go, and who is being left out?

    I've used satellite imagery to track how built-up land spreads, mapped the areas most at risk of flooding, and built web-GIS apps like EcoTrack at KNUST, where people report waste bins on a map so they get cleared faster.

    What drives me is turning location data into maps and apps that real people use, and that help planners decide where to act first.
