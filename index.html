The Analyst

The story behind the dashboards

So here's the deal: Engineer by training, then I discovered data analysis and realized I'd found my people (well, my spreadsheets). Turns out, I have an unhealthy obsession with making ugly data look gorgeous.

I've spent the last few years helping companies make sense of their chaos,from analyzing ad campaigns in Dubai to optimizing inventory for stores in Ghana.

When I'm not color-coding Power BI visualizations or arguing with Python about syntax errors, I'm plotting my world domination... I mean, my transition into data science/analytics engineering. Because why stop at making pretty charts when you can build machine learning models that actually predict the future? Or build clean reliable data pipelines
PS: Turns out I will still be an engineer
