Section 01
Overview

This performance analysis project breaks down a series of digital "Link Click" ad campaigns built to drive web traffic to an event landing page[cite: 22, 28]. As the project team lead, I directed a team of 8 analysts to examine the data[cite: 5, 8]. The system monitors ad performance across multiple active variations, helping management identify which individual ad assets are driving cost-efficient user actions and sign-ups[cite: 28, 30].

Section 02
Business Problem

The marketing team faced a common digital advertising challenge: some running ads were burning through the budget while bringing in almost zero results[cite: 38]. Without clear visual tracking, it was impossible to spot these failing campaigns[cite: 38]. The objective of this study was to isolate these low-performing, high-cost ad groups so the team could safely turn them off and back up these findings with visual charts for the finance department[cite: 39, 40].

Section 03
Methodology

The project analysis was executed using four clear steps:
• Explaining Key Abbreviations:
  - CTR (Click-Through Rate): The standard percentage of ad views that turn into actual clicks[cite: 31].
  - Unique CTR: The percentage of individual, distinct users who clicked the ad[cite: 45].
  - CPR (Cost Per Result): The exact amount of money spent to get a single desired user action[cite: 33].
• Data Cleaning & Math Check: Double-checked all incoming campaign logs to make sure cost numbers and click rates were accurately calculated across all datasets[cite: 44, 45].
• Visual Dashboard Build: Created scatter plots, custom line-bar charts, and bubble charts to clearly cross-compare campaign budgets against final engagement scores[cite: 47].

Section 04
Key Insights
⚠️

Failing Campaigns Isolated

Campaign 3 and Campaign 10 were flagged as the worst underperformers[cite: 53]. They pulled in the lowest amount of user clicks despite having the highest costs per result[cite: 53, 80].

📉

The Cost-Engagement Rule

The data proves a strong rule: as the cost to get a single result (CPR) climbs higher, actual unique click engagement drops sharply[cite: 55]. High costs equal poor audience interest[cite: 80].

✅

Top Performers to Scale

Campaign 2 and Campaign 8 were highlighted as highly successful, cost-efficient standouts[cite: 57]. They maintained very low costs while pulling in great audience interaction[cite: 57].
